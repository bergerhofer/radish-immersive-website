Style Seeker

Upload an image from your favorite TV show or movie and the app finds the pieces that make up the outfit—so you can buy them and look like your favorite character.

Type AI App
Built with Google AI Studio

Overview

Style Seeker is a project built with Google AI Studio. You upload an image from your favorite TV show or movie—a character in an outfit you love—and the app identifies the pieces that make up the look and helps you find them so you can purchase them and dress like that character.

It’s built for anyone who sees a fit on screen and wants to recreate it: the AI parses the outfit in your image and surfaces shoppable items so you can get the same style in real life.

How It Works

You provide a single image—a screenshot, promo shot, or photo from a show or film. The app uses AI to break down the outfit: tops, bottoms, shoes, accessories, and so on. From there it finds matching or similar items you can buy.

Flow

1. Upload — Drop an image of a character or person in the outfit you want.

2. Analyze — The app identifies the clothing and style details.

3. Shop — Get links or suggestions to purchase the pieces so you can look like your favorite character.

Tech Stack

Style Seeker was created using Google AI Studio, which powers the image understanding and outfit breakdown. The experience is designed so you can go from “I want that look” to “here’s how to get it” in a few steps.
