Agent Accessibility Audit

Can AI assistants find, understand and recommend your business? We score it out of 100, then show you what to fix.

Why it matters

More customers now ask an AI assistant who to hire, where to go or what to buy. The assistant answers from what it can read and verify on your website. When something is missing, it guesses, or it recommends someone it can verify.

Three questions

  • Can agents find you? Crawl access, sitemaps, canonical URLs, share images, llms.txt.
  • Can agents understand you? What you offer, for whom, where, who's behind it, and proof.
  • Can agents do something for your customers? Clear next steps, working forms and links, contact details.

Case study: Stingray Scout Drone Services

47 → 78
