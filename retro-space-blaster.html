Retro Space Blaster

A retro sci-fi blaster created with AI and brought into VR—inspired by vintage metal toy guns from the Toy and Miniature Museum.

Type XR / VR Sandbox
Inspiration Retro metal blaster guns
Capture Meta Quest 3

Overview

Retro Space Blaster started with a visit to the Toy and Miniature Museum, where I got inspired by retro metal blaster guns. I wanted to bring that look and feel into VR—so I created this blaster using AI and a short pipeline from image to 3D to headset.

Inspiration: retro metal blaster guns at the Toy and Miniature Museum

The result is a sci-fi blaster you can hold and use in VR: the design was generated as an image, turned into a 3D model with textures, then loaded into Quest and attached to the controller. The video on this page was captured through my Meta Quest 3.

How It Was Made

The pipeline is straightforward and repeatable:

Pipeline

1. Midjourney — Generate the image of the blaster.
2. Meshy — Create the 3D model and texture from the image.
3. Quest Building Blocks — Load the device and attach it to the controller (remote).
4. Meta Quest 3 — Capture the video in-headset.

So: concept and inspiration from the museum, visual design from AI image generation, geometry and surfacing from Meshy, and integration and capture in VR on the Quest. The blaster you see in the GIF is the same asset—held in hand and recorded directly from the headset.

Tech Stack

Each step uses a focused tool: Midjourney for the 2D concept, Meshy for image-to-3D and texturing, and Meta’s Quest Building Blocks to bring the model into VR and attach it to the controller. Video is captured natively on the Meta Quest 3.
