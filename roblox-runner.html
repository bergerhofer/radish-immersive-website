Roblox Runner

Control your Roblox character by moving your arms. Media Pipe tracks your pose and sends movement and turning to Roblox in real time.

Type Playable Story
Tech Media Pipe + Roblox

Overview

Roblox Runner is a Sandbox experiment that connects your real-world arm movements to your Roblox character. Using Media Pipe to track your arms, the system drives movement in Roblox: when you swing your arms, your player runs; when you hold up one hand, the character turns 90 degrees in that direction.

The result is a physical, full-body style of control—no keyboard or gamepad required. You run by pumping your arms and steer by raising a hand to turn left or right, so the experience feels closer to actually running and turning in space.

How It Works

A webcam (or camera feed) runs through Media Pipe, which detects your pose and arm positions. That data is mapped to two actions in Roblox:

Controls

Swing your arms — Your character runs. The motion of your arms is used to trigger and sustain forward movement.

Hold up one hand — Your character turns 90 degrees in that direction. Raise your left hand to turn left, right hand to turn right.

The bridge between Media Pipe and Roblox sends these signals in real time, so your on-screen character responds to your movements with minimal delay. Calibration and sensitivity can be tuned so it works in your play space and camera setup.

Tech Stack

The pipeline is: camera input → Media Pipe (pose/arm tracking) → a small bridge layer that converts gestures into run/turn commands → Roblox, where the player character is controlled.
