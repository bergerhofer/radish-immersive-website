Privacy policy

What we collect on this website, and what we do with it.

Effective 24 September 2026

What we collect

When you use the "Start with a question" form, we collect your name, email address, subject and message. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include.

How it's processed

Form submissions are sent through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service that forwards your message to our inbox. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use it

Only to reply to you and to discuss work you've asked us about. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to respond and for any work that follows. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@radishimmersive.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
