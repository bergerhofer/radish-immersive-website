The Opportunity

Find the opportunity hiding in plain sight.

Most organizations already have moments that work.

People show up. They participate. They linger. They come back. They tell someone else. Employees find ways around broken processes. Customers behave differently than anyone expected.

Those moments contain clues.

The Radish Team studies how people actually experience your organization, not just how the experience was designed to work. We talk with the people inside it, observe the people using it, and look for the gap between intention and reality.

  • They show up and participate
  • They linger — and they come back
  • They tell someone else
  • Employees route around broken processes
  • Customers behave differently than expected

Then we ask: what do people need enough context and meaning to confidently do differently?

How We Work

From lived experience to a tested intervention.

Research is a short path to action.

01

Observe

We spend time inside the real experience — observing behavior and talking with customers, visitors, employees and leadership to find the moments, barriers and existing behaviors that matter.

02

Interpret

We turn what we've learned into a point of view, identifying the strongest opportunities and shaping interventions around distinctiveness, feasibility and business potential.

03

Make

We build something — a new experience, service, interaction, environment, story, digital product, intelligent technology, or a combination. The medium follows the opportunity.

04

Test

We put it into the world with real people and measure what happens, what changes, and what the organization learns — then recommend what to expand, change, or stop.

Then we recommend what to expand, change, or stop.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What does Radish Immersive do?

Radish Immersive is an experience design studio. We help organizations create physical and digital experiences that change what people feel, do, discover, remember and share, combining human observation, research, storytelling, experience design, emerging technology and rapid experimentation.

Who do you work with?

Organizations that run places, programs and moments people take part in: attractions, museums and cultural venues, hospitality, events and entertainment, retail and brand spaces, and workplaces.

What does an engagement look like?

We follow four steps: Observe, Interpret, Make, Test. We study the real experience, form a point of view, make something, then test it with real people and measure what changed. You can start with research alone or bring a specific problem.

Do we need to know what we want built?

No. Bring a place, experience, audience, behavior or business problem you're curious about. Working out what deserves to exist is part of the job.

How do you measure success?

Before we make anything, we agree on the behavior worth changing, such as visits, participation, engagement, spending or return rate. Then we measure it in a live test.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the scope, and we won't invent a business case before we understand the business. Every engagement starts with a conversation, followed by a scoped first phase.

Where are you based?

Radish Immersive is based in the Kansas City area.

What is an Agent Accessibility Audit?

A scored review of how well AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity can find, understand and act on your website, with a prioritized fix list. See the audit.
