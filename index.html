The Opportunity

Find the opportunity hiding in plain sight.

Most organizations already have moments that work.

People show up. They participate. They linger. They come back. They tell someone else. Employees find ways around broken processes. Customers behave differently than anyone expected.

Those moments contain clues.

The Radish Team studies how people actually experience your organization, not just how the experience was designed to work. We talk with the people inside it, observe the people using it, and look for the gap between intention and reality.

  • They show up and participate
  • They linger — and they come back
  • They tell someone else
  • Employees route around broken processes
  • Customers behave differently than expected

Then we ask: what do people need enough context and meaning to confidently do differently?

How We Work

From lived experience to a tested intervention.

Research is a short path to action.

01

Observe

We spend time inside the real experience — observing behavior and talking with customers, visitors, employees and leadership to find the moments, barriers and existing behaviors that matter.

02

Interpret

We turn what we've learned into a point of view, identifying the strongest opportunities and shaping interventions around distinctiveness, feasibility and business potential.

03

Make

We build something — a new experience, service, interaction, environment, story, digital product, intelligent technology, or a combination. The medium follows the opportunity.

04

Test

We put it into the world with real people and measure what happens, what changes, and what the organization learns — then recommend what to expand, change, or stop.

Then we recommend what to expand, change, or stop.
